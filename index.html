Questions

Do I need a smartwatch?

No. THRUM reads Garmin and Apple Health, and works without any tracker through a 30-second check-in.

What will it cost?

We plan a one-time price for the machine and a monthly powder plan, with a cheaper starter plan. People on the waitlist hear the price first.

What happens with my data?

We read only what the dose needs, with your consent, and you can see and delete everything. Your email is used for THRUM updates only.
